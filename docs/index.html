Community Motorcycle Sale & Repair Operations

ISIC 4540 · maturity unknown

Open Business Blueprint for ISIC Rev.5 4540: sale, maintenance and repair of motorcycles and related parts and accessories.

This repository designs a forkable OSS business for community motorcycle dealership/workshop operations coordination: sale/repair- order and parts-used logging, bay/technician service scheduling, safety-concern flagging, and parts/inventory procurement coordination -- run by a qualified operator so a dealership/workshop keeps its own auditable service and safety-concern history instead of a closed shop-management platform.

Motorcycle repair/maintenance has a direct road-safety dimension: a badly serviced motorcycle can kill its rider. This actor deliberately does NOT hold roadworthiness-clearance authority -- motorcycleops.governor HARD-blocks, permanently, any proposal that attempts to finalize a roadworthiness-clearance decision (certifying a motorcycle safe to return to the road/customer after service or sale), no matter the op, no matter the confidence. That decision always stays with a qualified human technician/inspector. This actor coordinates the back office around that decision; it never makes it.
